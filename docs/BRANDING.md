# GoreeCloud Notebook branding

**Product name:** GoreeCloud Notebook. **Category:** Personal knowledge and note management. **Position:** A self-hosted Evernote alternative. **Identity status:** Development; rebranding across runtime, packaging, icons, help and extension naming is incomplete.

Visual and interaction authority belongs to **Glaze — GoreeCloud Design & Experience System**, current target V1.7 / 1.7.0. Notebook's product-specific botanical green accent is an adaptation, not a platform-wide default or a competing design-system authority. Use solid, readable content surfaces, restrained translucent navigation, semantic focus/critical/status colors, and keyboard/reduced-motion/forced-colors accessibility.

Retain original TriliumNext attribution and AGPL notices. Do not falsely imply the upstream team has endorsed or certified GoreeCloud Notebook. Asset/icon migration must preserve third-party rights and provide provenance.
