# GoreeCloud Notebook
## Complete Features and Capabilities

**Product:** GoreeCloud Notebook  
**Category:** Note-Taking, Knowledge Management, Second Brain, Document Management, and Productivity  
**Feature Scope:** Comprehensive Product Vision

### Product Overview

GoreeCloud Notebook is designed to be a comprehensive, privacy-focused, self-hostable digital notebook and knowledge management platform.

It combines powerful note-taking, document editing, information capture, hierarchical organization, interconnected knowledge, structured databases, task management, visual planning, automation, artificial intelligence, and secure information storage into one integrated application.

GoreeCloud Notebook is intended to serve as a centralized workspace for personal knowledge, professional documentation, research, project management, and long-term information preservation.

The following defines the proposed complete feature and capability set, including foundational and advanced functionality. It is a product vision rather than a claim that every feature has already been implemented.

## GoreeCloud Notebook — Core Product Principles

The full feature set is guided by the following product principles:

1. **Privacy by Default:** Notes, notebooks, workspaces, and attachments remain private unless explicitly shared.
2. **User Data Ownership:** Users retain the ability to access, export, migrate, back up, and recover their information.
3. **Independent Hosting:** Core functionality must not depend on a proprietary cloud service.
4. **Knowledge Interconnection:** Notes, documents, tasks, projects, databases, and relationships operate within a unified knowledge system.
5. **Flexible Organization:** Hierarchical notebooks, tags, metadata, relationships, and database views coexist without forcing a single organizational method.
6. **Powerful Editing:** Markdown, rich text, structured blocks, diagrams, and specialized content types provide flexible ways to create information.
7. **Extensibility:** Plugins, APIs, automation, and configurable integrations allow the application to evolve.
8. **Optional Artificial Intelligence:** AI enhances productivity without becoming a mandatory dependency.
9. **Reliable Portability and Recovery:** Open export formats, verified backups, and tested restoration procedures protect long-term access to information.
10. **Controlled Collaboration:** Shared information remains governed by explicit permissions and secure access controls.
11. **Cross-Platform Accessibility:** Users can access their knowledge through suitable desktop, mobile, and browser experiences as platform clients are developed.
12. **Long-Term Maintainability:** The software architecture prioritizes maintainability, documented interfaces, testability, recoverability, and independent development.

## Product Definition

**GoreeCloud Notebook is a comprehensive digital notebook, second-brain, knowledge-management, and productivity platform designed to unify information capture, rich document editing, hierarchical organization, interconnected knowledge, structured databases, task management, visual planning, automation, intelligent assistance, collaboration, and secure data ownership.**

Its long-term objective is to provide one extensible, privately controlled environment for collecting, creating, organizing, connecting, retrieving, managing, and preserving personal and professional knowledge.
